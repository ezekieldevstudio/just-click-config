Just Click FAQ

Quick answers for Just Click v1.

What is Just Click?

Just Click is a simple counter, pace, and interval tool.

How many counters can I use?

You can use up to four counters at once. Each has its own count, name, and optional goal. Use the + and − controls to adjust a count. Counts cannot go below zero.

How do I reset a counter?

Open the counter’s three-dot options menu and select Reset count. This resets that counter to zero without removing it. The same menu lets you edit its name and goal or remove it, as long as at least one counter remains.

What is Pace mode?

In v1, Pace is a settings and state preview. You can set the starting interval, minimum interval, speed-up frequency, and total repetitions, and use Start, Pause, Resume, and Reset. Automatic repetition counting, automatic speed changes, and sound are not active yet.

What is Interval mode?

Interval alternates between timed WORK and REST phases for the number of rounds you choose. You can start, pause, resume, and reset the timer. Set rest to zero to skip it. There is no rest after the final round. The timer pauses when you leave the app and does not play sounds.

Are my counts and settings saved?

Counts, counter names, goals, and workout settings are held in the current app session. They are not saved as a permanent workout history. Closing or reloading the app clears them; the operating system may also end the session while the app is in the background.

Is my data sent to a server?

Counter inputs and workout settings are processed locally in the app session. Just Click does not send them to EzekielDevStudio servers and does not require an account or login. Opening a help or policy link uses your external browser.

Does Just Click show ads?

No. The current version has no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.
