Just Click Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026
This policy describes the current Just Click app, developed by EzekielDevStudio.

Information handled by the app

Just Click does not require account creation or login. The app does not collect personal or sensitive user data for EzekielDevStudio.

Counter names, counts, goals, and workout settings you enter are processed locally on your device during the current app session. They are not transmitted to EzekielDevStudio servers. Just Click has no developer-operated server.

Session data and deletion

The current version holds your inputs and workout settings in session memory. It does not provide permanent local workout history, cloud storage, or account synchronization. Closing or reloading the app clears the session; it may also be lost if the operating system ends the app in the background.

You can reset counters and timers within the app. Uninstalling the app removes app-local data managed by the operating system. EzekielDevStudio has no server-side workout records to retain or delete.

Advertising, analytics, and tracking

The current app has no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. EzekielDevStudio does not sell or share your in-app inputs or workout settings with advertisers or analytics services.

External pages and email

Help and privacy links open in your external browser. Visiting a web page sends ordinary web requests to its hosting provider; the browser and hosting provider may handle technical information under their own policies. This does not upload your counter inputs or workout settings.

If you choose to email us, your email address and message are received through our email provider and used to respond to your request. This is separate from data processed locally by the app. Please do not include sensitive information that is not needed for support.

Changes to this policy

Future versions may change how data is handled. If those practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date to describe them.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact EzekielDevStudio at ezekieldevstudio@gmail.com.
